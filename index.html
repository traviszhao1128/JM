Travis (Xiaoyu) Zhao, CFA

Ph.D. Candidate in Finance · Isenberg School of Management, University of Massachusetts Amherst

I study how financial markets absorb information that is material but hard to quantify — geopolitical conflict and unscheduled corporate events — and how sophisticated investors identify, price, and trade on that information. My current work examines how institutional investors trade around tariff-induced events, hedge fund exposure to U.S.–China tension, and informed option trading around cybersecurity incidents.

Before my doctoral studies, I have worked in investment management and banking in Hong Kong and Beijing, China. I am a Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) charterholder. I am on the 2026–27 academic job market.
