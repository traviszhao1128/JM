Travis (Xiaoyu) Zhao, CFA

Ph.D. Candidate in Finance · Isenberg School of Management, University of Massachusetts Amherst

I study how financial markets absorb information that is material but hard to quantify — geopolitical conflict and unscheduled corporate events — and how sophisticated investors identify, price, and trade on that information. My current work examines hedge fund exposure to U.S.–China tension and informed option trading around cybersecurity incidents.

Before my doctoral studies, I spent three years in investment management and banking in Hong Kong and Beijing. I am a Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) charterholder. I expect to defend my dissertation in Spring 2027 and am on the 2026–27 academic job market, available to start in August 2027.

Job Market Paper

Hedge Fund Performance and the U.S.–China Tension

Funds with higher exposure to U.S.–China tension earn significantly higher out-of-sample excess returns and risk-adjusted alphas, and directional funds actively time the tension — evidence of skill rather than passive risk compensation.
